### About me

I'm a high-school student from Tunisia (Informatique track, bac 2027–2028) who describes himself as **a programmer and a poet** — long-term goal: deep mastery of programming languages, and keeping creative writing alive alongside the code.

Right now I explore **applied AI for real domains** — health informatics, agriculture, flood and climate risk — through small, honest projects and independent research. My operating principle, from my published review on clinical decision-support robustness, is **honesty engineering**: a system that can't say *"I don't know"* shouldn't be deciding.

### Research

**[AI-Based Clinical Decision-Support System Robustness Under Unreliable Patient Data: A Narrative Review with Thematic Synthesis](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22883408)** — sole author, published on Zenodo (CERN/EU repository), 2026-09-21, CC-BY-4.0.

15 studies (2018–2026), six robustness themes, and the conceptual **CARE framework**. Central finding: robustness is conditional, not absolute — and the honest limitations are stated in the paper itself.
